## The Friction Point

Finding a consistent playing partner is a real friction point in recreational sports. SquashNoFriends is a scheduling platform that matches squash players based on their availability. Users describe their free time in plain language, and the system handles the rest.

## Technical Execution

I wanted to demonstrate how natural language processing can remove barriers to product adoption—users shouldn't have to fill out rigid forms if they can just describe their schedule naturally.

- Built an NLP parser that converts free-text availability descriptions into structured time intervals.
- Designed a Python scheduling algorithm that maximizes optimal pairings while respecting time constraints.
- Developed the full stack using a Next.js frontend and a MySQL backend.
- Shipped the working prototype during a 24-hour hackathon environment.

This project proves my ability to scope, design, and deliver a working product under extreme time pressure. It successfully combines NLP, algorithm design, and full-stack development into a practical tool.
